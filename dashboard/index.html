Honesty notes

  • The board says a protocol has “no token on record” — that is what the snapshot records, not proof it has no token. A token could exist under a name the snapshot does not carry.
  • An implied value is the model’s claim, not a promise: it is TVL times a market multiple, adjusted for growth, maturity and chains. Markets can disagree by a lot.
  • Live tokens are kept apart on purpose. Their prices are the market’s own values; mixing them into the ranking would let traded tokens set the answer they are meant to evidence.
  • Liquid-staking and liquid-restaking protocols are ranked: their categories have launched tokens before. Bridges are excluded: a bridge moves other tokens, it does not wait for its own launch.

Token Radar ranks pre-token protocols by an implied launch value and gives each one a seven-step farm plan. The model is stated in full under Methodology; the numbers come from a frozen snapshot, not from a live feed.

Nothing here is investment advice. Farming a protocol does not guarantee an airdrop, and a token that launches can be worth far less than the model claims — or nothing at all. Never pay anyone to claim for you.
